One of India's largest non-bank lenders

Bajaj Finance Limited is a non-banking financial company (NBFC) serving millions of customers across consumer, SME, commercial, rural, mortgage and deposit businesses. It is a subsidiary of Bajaj Finserv Limited, which is a separate listed company.

Its philosophy is simple: make credit accessible, fast and transparent, using data and technology at scale.

Vision

To be a trusted, technology-led financial partner for every customer segment.

Mission

Deliver simple, fast and transparent financial solutions.

Values

Customer focus, integrity, innovation and accountability.

Vision, mission and values are summarised for this showcase; see official sources for exact wording.
